# Third-Party Notices

## F1 Constructors Color Codes Collection

OEM Paint Lab includes 22 adapted 2026 Formula 1 constructor color records based on the F1 Constructors Color Codes Collection. The adapted data includes constructor associations, primary and secondary color roles, and digital HEX references.

Some color labels were normalized for OEM Paint Lab, and a small number of factual season notes were paraphrased. Record-level source links are retained within the dataset.

Source repository:
[https://github.com/Mahshadn/f1-constructors-colour-codes](https://github.com/Mahshadn/f1-constructors-colour-codes)

Source data:
[https://github.com/Mahshadn/f1-constructors-colour-codes/blob/main/2026-season.md](https://github.com/Mahshadn/f1-constructors-colour-codes/blob/main/2026-season.md)

OEM Paint Lab’s software, interface design, database schema, provenance model, calculated color representations, validation pipeline, and presentation are independent work.
